Housing is eating London’s weekly budget alive

Weekly household spending on housing, fuel & power by region — three-year average FYE 2023–25. The gap between London and the rest is not a rounding error; it is a second household budget.

London households spend £182.20 a week on housing, fuel and power — 62% above the UK average. That single premium is almost enough to cover the average UK household’s entire weekly food bill.
