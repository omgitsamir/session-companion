England's Two Health Nations

How deprivation writes a death sentence across 153 local authorities. Explore the scatter: life expectancy vs. under-75 mortality, coloured by the social gradient that drives them.

In Blackpool, the most deprived place in England, a man can expect to live 9.3 fewer years than a man in leafy Wokingham. That gap is not fate — it is policy. Drag the chart, search your area, and switch the mortality lens: cardiovascular disease, cancer, or all-cause. The pattern barely changes. Poverty is the strongest predictor of who dies early.
