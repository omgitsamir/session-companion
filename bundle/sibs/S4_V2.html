London's Housing Crisis in Numbers

How the capital's households spend £182/week on housing — more than twice the North East

💷 The Burden Nobody Talks About

London households spend £182.20 per week on housing, fuel and power — that's 26% of their total weekly budget. Meanwhile, households in the North East spend just £82.10. This isn't just a cost-of-living crisis; it's a geography-of-opportunity crisis.
